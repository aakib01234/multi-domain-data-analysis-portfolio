# Project 1 Business Recommendations

1. **Inventory:** Raise safety stock for the best category-product combinations before peak periods; monitor weekly sell-through and stock-outs.
2. **Promotions:** Test weekend and evening bundles instead of blanket discounts, using a control branch to measure incremental profit.
3. **Customer retention:** Offer member-exclusive cross-category bundles and track repeat visits, average basket value, and margin.
4. **Operations:** Align staffing and shelf replenishment with the strongest weekday-hour combinations.
5. **Performance management:** Maintain a weekly scorecard covering revenue, profit, margin, stock-outs, basket value, and repeat purchase rate.

These recommendations are hypotheses derived from simulated observational data. A controlled pilot should precede full rollout.
