# Portfolio Overview

## Objective

Build a five-domain portfolio that demonstrates an end-to-end analytical workflow: data quality assessment, cleaning, transformation, descriptive statistics, trend and correlation analysis, visualization, and actionable recommendations.

## Project 1 summary

The Supermarket Sales Analysis evaluates transactions across branches, categories, customer segments, payment methods, weekdays, and shopping hours. The project combines revenue and profit KPIs with operational recommendations for inventory, promotion timing, and customer retention.

## Project 2 summary

The Student Performance Analysis evaluates pass/fail rates, subject-level outcomes, attendance-performance correlation, study habits, demographic comparisons, and early-warning risk indicators. It translates statistical evidence into targeted academic interventions.

## Project 3 summary

The Weather Data Analysis studies three years of daily observations across six Indian cities. It identifies temperature trends, monsoon rainfall concentration, seasonal differences, humidity relationships, and city-level extreme-weather exposure.

## Project 4 summary

The Healthcare Treatment Effectiveness Analysis uses simulated, de-identified encounters to compare clinical improvement, outcomes, length of stay, 30-day readmission, cost, and satisfaction. Results are stratified by condition and interpreted as descriptive associations rather than causal treatment effects.

## Project 5 summary

The Stock Market and Portfolio Risk Analysis evaluates simulated daily prices for five fictional assets. It measures compounded return, annualised volatility, risk-adjusted return, maximum drawdown, correlations, and equal-weight portfolio diversification.

## Tools

Python, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook, ReportLab, and pytest.
