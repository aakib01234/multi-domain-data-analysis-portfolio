# Portfolio Demo Script

## Recommended duration: 7-9 minutes

### 1. Introduction - 30 seconds

“This portfolio contains five end-to-end data-analysis projects across retail, education, weather, healthcare, and finance. I used a consistent workflow covering data quality, transformation, statistics, visualization, recommendations, testing, and executive reporting.”

### 2. Repository structure - 45 seconds

Open `README.md`. Explain the separation of raw/clean data, notebooks, reusable source functions, build scripts, tests, reports, visualizations, and documentation.

### 3. Retail - 60 seconds

Show category revenue and daily trend. Explain net sales/profit engineering and how peak periods translate into inventory, promotion, and staffing actions.

### 4. Education - 60 seconds

Show subject performance and attendance correlation. Explain the pass rule, at-risk flag, and why flags support teacher review rather than punishment.

### 5. Weather - 60 seconds

Show monthly temperature and seasonal heatmap. Explain city-level differences and why simplified thresholds are not official warnings.

### 6. Healthcare - 75 seconds

Show stratified improvement and readmission. Emphasise simulated de-identified data, confounding, descriptive interpretation, and clinician oversight.

### 7. Finance - 75 seconds

Show cumulative return, risk-return, and drawdown. Explain annualisation, the 6% risk-free assumption, equal weighting, and why simulated performance is not advice.

### 8. Testing - 45 seconds

Run `python -m pytest -q` and show `15 passed`. Explain that tests use small known datasets to verify cleaning, feature engineering, flags, and KPI calculations.

### 9. Conclusion - 30 seconds

“The value of the portfolio is not only the charts; it is the reproducible pipeline that turns imperfect raw data into validated evidence, transparent assumptions, and responsible recommendations.”
